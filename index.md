Welcome to my **LIBR 509 – Foundations of Resource Description and Knowledge Organization** ePortfolio.

This site is a record of how I learned to *see* information systems differently – not just as neutral tools, but as designs shaped by values, labour, and communities.

---

## About this portfolio

This ePortfolio brings together my work for LIBR 509, including:

- **Weekly notes** – key concepts from lectures and readings, in my own words  
- **In-class & group work** – design exercises, experiments, and conversations  
- **Assignments & peer review** – my submissions, feedback received, and revisions  
- **Weekly reflections** – what challenged me, what clicked, and how I’m improving  

Rather than only showing “final” products, I want this space to trace my **thinking process**:  
how my understanding of classification, metadata, vocabularies, and systems integration develops over time.

---

## Who I am (in this course)

I am a first-year **MLIS student at UBC iSchool**, interested in:

- metadata and cataloguing  
- book and manuscript history  
- how information systems reflect power, identity, and community needs  

LIBR 509 is one of the first courses where I began to connect these interests to concrete design work:  
designing small classifications and vocabularies, analysing real library systems, and reflecting on what “good” description might mean in practice.
